How It Works
Predict · Explain · Recommend

A four-stage pipeline that goes beyond a binary result to deliver clinical-grade explainability and actionable guidance.

🔍
Predict
Deep learning neural network classifies risk as Low, Moderate, or Very High using 14 routine clinical features.
🧠
Explain
SHAP identifies the top 10 clinical factors driving each individual prediction — red increases risk, green reduces it.
💊
Recommend
Personalized health recommendations mapped from the patient's top SHAP risk contributors to actionable clinical advice.
📊
Visualise
Dark-themed interactive GUI with sliders and dropdowns — designed for non-specialists and rural health workers.
Risk Classification
3-Tier Risk Output

Every prediction is classified into one of three tiers with a confidence score and raw probability.

✓ Low Risk
Probability < 0.40
⚠ Moderate Risk
Probability 0.40 – 0.60
🔴 Very High Risk
Probability > 0.60
Features
What Makes This Different
🧠
SHAP Explainable AI
Per-patient feature contribution chart — not a global explanation, but individual to every prediction.
💊
Personalized Recommendations
Health guidance mapped from the top SHAP risk contributor to plain-language clinical action items.
⚖️
SMOTE Class Balancing
Synthetic oversampling applied on train set only — corrects class imbalance without data leakage.
🎯
Dynamic Threshold
Optimal decision boundary found via accuracy sweep from 0.30 to 0.70 — not defaulted to 0.5.
📋
Full Evaluation Suite
Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, Confusion Matrix, ROC Curve — all computed and visualised.
🌐
Zero Drive Dependency
Dataset loaded directly from GitHub repo via git clone — runs on any Colab session instantly.

Neural Network Design

↓
Dense(32, ReLU)
↓

Adam lr=0.0005
Binary Crossentropy
EarlyStopping val_auc

100 Max Epochs
Batch Size 32
